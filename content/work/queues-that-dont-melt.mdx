## Context

A CRM that sends campaigns lives or dies by its queues. Ours ran campaigns, drip sequences, cron automations, bulk imports and every outbound email through BullMQ on Redis.

The interesting part was learning that per-worker limits add up: with *concurrency × workers × per-worker limit*, a "safe" setting on one worker becomes an unsafe one across the fleet. The fix was one shared budget for the whole system.

The full write-up (the two-lane sender model, the suspension post-mortem, domain authentication and inbound reply routing) is being written now.
